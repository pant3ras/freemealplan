## Full description (max 4000)
<!-- full:en -->
WeekPlate plans your week of meals, tells you what to buy and what it will cost, and helps you decide what to cook tonight.

PLAN THE WEEK IN SECONDS
• Breakfasts, lunches, soups, dinners and snacks built around your daily calorie and protein targets
• Portions adjusted to each day so the numbers add up
• Swipe the cuisines you're craving and the week follows
• Swap any meal with a swipe, or remove it and let the rest of the day make up for it

DECIDE TOGETHER
• Can't agree on dinner? You and your partner swipe the same dishes on your own phones
• Send a short code by message and see the dishes you both like
• Cook the match tonight, or plan the whole week around it

WHAT TO COOK TODAY?
• See today's lunch and dinner at a glance
• Swipe for something else, or let "Surprise me" pick a dish that fits your day

SHOP SMARTER
• One shopping list, grouped by aisle, with real pack sizes
• See which supermarket is cheapest for your basket: Kaufland, Lidl, Carrefour, Mega Image or Selgros, using official shelf prices
• Cook from what you already have: tick what's in the fridge and the plan uses it up

COOK WITH CONFIDENCE
• 185 recipes with photos: Romanian classics, Italian, Greek, Mediterranean, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Korean, Indian, Mexican, American and Middle Eastern cooking, plus soups, desserts and quick weeknight meals
• Step-by-step methods, hands-on and total times, and a servings control to scale any recipe
• Batch cooking: cook once on Sunday and the plan tells you how to split the boxes
• Add your own recipes and they are planned, priced and shopped like the built-in ones

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your data stays on your phone. The app does not even ask for internet access

English and Romanian. Nutrition from USDA FoodData Central. Prices from the Monitorul Prețurilor price monitor. Food photos from Wikimedia Commons, credited in the app.

Enjoying WeekPlate? You can support it with a coffee from the Me tab, or send a recipe or feedback from there.

## Data safety form
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No**
  - The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything itself.
  - Profile, plan, recipes and match codes are stored only on the device.
  - The feedback and recipe forms open the user's own mail app; the user sends the email themselves, which Google does not count as collection by the app.
  - The "Decide together" code is shared by the user through their own messaging app and holds only yes/no swipes and an optional first name.
- Is all data encrypted in transit? Not applicable (no data transmitted)
- Do you provide a way for users to request deletion? Yes: "Reset app" in the Me tab, or uninstall
